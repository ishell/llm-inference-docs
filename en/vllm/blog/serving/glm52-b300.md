# GLM-5.2 on 24×B300: SLA, not peak TPS; TPOT 40ms→17ms

Chinese: `../../zh/vllm/blog/serving/glm52-b300.md`  
3×8 B300, 4P1D. GLM-5.2-NVFP4 (744B / 40B active). Mean TTFT ≤2.5s, mean TPOT ≤20ms.

P/D is the starting point: colocated long prompts stretch ITL of in-flight decode, so a TPOT SLA cannot be signed. The biggest cut was not a kernel — first decode step after KV handoff is 1 token; MTP requests already run 1+N; mixed batch drops full CUDA Graph. Under DP, one rank receiving a new request pulls all ranks. Dummy speculative padding (PR #45237) equalizes shape: **~40ms → ~22ms**.

Then: MoE needs `VLLM_USE_V2_MODEL_RUNNER=1`; MRV2 another ~**11%** TPOT (DSA indexer warmup, MTP local argmax, dynamic spec + full graph). FlashInfer NVLink A2A `flashinfer_nvlink_two_sided` ~**4%**. Decode `--compilation-config '{"cudagraph_mode":"FULL_DECODE_ONLY"}'`. MTP decode `num_speculative_tokens=3`, prefill 1. IndexerCache shares DSA Top-K across MTP steps; without it, re-indexing eats the speculation.

Prefill: TP1 DP2 EP wins TGS; they shipped **TP1 DP4 EP** — ~8% per-GPU for four-GPU KV and 1M context. Highest TGS is not the SLA config. PCP virtual batching may reopen that trade. Treat MTP acceptance as a first-class alert. Long soak found `new_block_ids` recorded but never drained on non-Mamba models; host RSS grew linearly (#47723 / #44490). v0.26.0 has the holes they hit.
